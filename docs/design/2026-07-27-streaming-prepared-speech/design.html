Streaming Prepared Speech: Sentence-Level Compilation on the Phone Line

2026-07-27 · branch call-review-panel · proposed by David; architecture refined to recompute-with-frontier · markdown source: design.md

Problem

The phone's "prepared" speech mode buffered the entire LLM reply before compiling it into SpeechChunks (pauses, comma/dash clause expansion, spoken-form normalization) — the caller waited for full generation (~2.3s) + prep + first synthesis after STT before hearing anything.

Invariant: for ANY partitioning of a reply into deltas, streaming output is IDENTICAL to batch compile_speech(whole) (jitter=0) — texts, kinds, pauses, sequences, list numbering, label merges, normalizations. Enforced by the partition-fuzz golden suite in tests/python/test_streaming_speech_compiler.py.

Architecture: recompute-with-frontier

StreamingSpeechCompiler keeps only the raw buffer + emitted-unit texts. Every feed(delta) re-runs the exact batch pipeline over the whole buffer (pure regex, microseconds) and emits units up to a conservative stability frontier — document-level state (list counters, label merges, paragraph joins) is recomputed from scratch, so equivalence holds by construction. The frontier holds back:

  1. The open tail region (trailing paragraph — even newline-terminated, the next line would join it; a heading/list line missing its newline; a bare trailing \r).
  2. The last sentence of an open paragraph (no normalization pattern spans two sentence terminals; the held sentence absorbs single-boundary patterns like No. 5, e.g., dotted acronyms).
  3. A closed label-like segment ≤10 words with no follower yet (batch label-merge may claim it).
  4. An open tail with unclosed markup (odd backtick/fence/asterisk counts, unbalanced brackets).

Streamed chunks always get punctuation-table pauses; only finish()'s last chunk carries the 140 ms transport tail. Emission is strictly in order with one jitter draw per non-final chunk — the batch draw sequence, seeded-testable. finish(final_text) has a longest-common-prefix guard: on divergence it emits only beyond the emitted count and logs — never re-speaks, never crashes.

Phone integration

Three-way mode via NANO_CLAW_PHONE_SPEECH_PREPARATION (console-settable, persisted): 1 = streaming prepared (default), batch = old buffer-all escape hatch, raw = no compiler. Server-rewrite safety: a giant first delta (>350 chars — the held-response synthetic delta) or a deep_started event makes the turn compile at final only; at final, a diverging response after chunks were spoken raises the prepared_stream_mismatch tap event, sets preparedStreamMismatch: true on the turn payload, and withholds the rewritten tail (never double-speak). Extensions flow through finish(response) naturally. The thinking cue stops at first speech — its window shortens automatically.

Bundled fix: reload flake family

Pause/clause/jitter knobs are now read per call instead of frozen at import; every importlib.reload was deleted from test_speech_preparer.py (reloading rebound the SpeechChunk class and order-poisoned other test files). SPEECH_COMPILER_VERSION stays nanoclaw-speech-v1. The remaining test_history_api failure is a deterministic pre-existing breakage, unrelated and out of scope.

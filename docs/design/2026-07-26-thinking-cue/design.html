Thinking Cue: Ack Chime + Clock Ticking During the STT/LLM Wait

2026-07-26 · branch call-review-panel · approved by David (sound: clock ticking; timing: ack blip + repeat) · markdown source: design.md

Problem

After the caller stops speaking, the line is silent for the whole thinking window — STT (~5s on whisper/medium) + LLM (~2.8s hedged) + first-sentence synthesis. In the 2026-07-26 13:30 incident call the caller asked "are you there?" and hung up. Silence reads as a dead line.

Caller experience

  • The instant an utterance is accepted, the existing soft two-note chime plays — "I heard you."
  • While the turn thinks, a quiet clock tick (~35 ms damped 2 kHz sine, ≈ −28 dBFS) plays every 0.5 s.
  • Ticking stops the moment any real speech starts, on barge-in, turn end, or hangup.

Design

  1. voice/processing_audio.py — thinking_tick(): lru-cached 48 kHz PCM16 tick, click-free attack/decay; processing_chime() reused unchanged as the ack.
  2. voice/phone.py — per-turn cue task, independent of the sentence pipeline: _start_thinking_cue() at the top of _run_turn; _stop_thinking_cue() from _speak_sentences, _interrupt, _turn_finished, close(). _play_cue() converts via the existing codec frame helpers, paces with its own FramePacer, tees tap.outbound_frame (cues appear in recordings and /calls), never touches self.speaking. Stop-event checked before every frame — cue audio can never interleave with reply audio. Gate: NANO_CLAW_PHONE_THINKING_CUE=off (console-overridable, persisted). Tap events thinking_cue_start/stop with tick count.
  3. Billing — none: cues bypass _synthesize_sentence, consistent with the existing earcon rule. Scheduler-flow turns share _run_turn → same cue.

Not doing

No volume/interval console controls; no per-phase sounds; no cue during greeting or idle prompts.

Tests & verification

Tick waveform properties; cue loop vs a recording fake websocket (ack immediately, ticks accumulate, stop halts, off disables, idempotent stop); _speak_sentences stops the cue before speech. Then: pytest → rebuild → drain/undrain → loopback ("FIRST ANSWER AUDIO") → tap shows thinking_cue_start, ticks ≥ 1, thinking_cue_stop before sentence-1 playback → live call to 512-277-7311.
